Evangelion: 3.0 You Can (Not) Redo

2012 Rebuild of Evangelion Movie 03 Anime / Mecha / Drama

The third film in the Rebuild of Evangelion series takes the story into a darker and more distant future. Shinji Ikari awakens in a world that has changed completely, where former allies no longer trust him and the consequences of the past are impossible to ignore. The movie shifts the tone toward isolation, confusion, loss, and the heavy price of choice.
